# NM-5067 - Commissions > Tier/Flat: the states only a real save shows
# (sub-ticket NM-3350). Run on 1101 (Audio Visual, USD): the message of a
# saved tier, the grid after the save, January chosen in Reset Dates, and the
# message its Save shows.
# Run: node scripts/generic-testid-audit.js --steps ../NM-5067/config/audit-steps/commissions/nm-5067-tier-flat-save.txt --office 1101
#
# Changes data, then puts it back: Comm % of the APP Downloaded tier is saved
# as 1 % and then as 0 % again (History keeps an entry for each save). The
# reset dates are not stored for 1101: the months stay empty.

Module: Commissions
Submodule: tier-flat-save-nm-5067
Output: reports/testid-audit-commissions
Elements: all

1. Go to /navigator/locations/{office}/commissions/tiers
2. Wait for "css=main button:text-is('Search'):not([disabled])"
3. Wait until the page settles
4. Wait 2 seconds
5. Click "css=main button:text-is('Search')"
6. Wait up to 90 seconds for "css=main tbody tr [data-testid^=commission-tier-delete-]"
7. Wait until the page settles
# A tier saved: Comm % of APP Downloaded to 1 %.
8. Click "css=main tbody tr:has-text('APP Downloaded') [data-testid^=commission-tier-commission-]"
9. Type "1" into "css=main tbody tr:has-text('APP Downloaded') input" and leave
10. Click "css=main button:text-is('Save')"
11. Audit message as "Tiers saved message"
12. Wait until the page settles
13. Audit inside "main" as "Search done - tier saved"
# Put back: 0 %.
14. Hover "css=main h1"
15. Wait 8 seconds
16. Click "css=main tbody tr:has-text('APP Downloaded') [data-testid^=commission-tier-commission-]"
17. Type "0" into "css=main tbody tr:has-text('APP Downloaded') input" and leave
18. Click "css=main button:text-is('Save')"
19. Wait for "css=[data-sonner-toast]"
20. Wait until the page settles
# Reset dates: January = 1 and Save; the office keeps no reset dates, so nothing
# needs putting back.
21. Hover "css=main h1"
22. Wait 8 seconds
23. Click "css=main button:has-text('Reset Dates')"
24. Wait for "css=[role=dialog] [role=combobox]"
25. Wait until the page settles
26. Click "css=[role=dialog] [role=combobox] >> nth=0"
27. Click "css=[role=listbox] [role=option] >> nth=1"
28. Audit inside "[role=dialog]" as "Reset - January chosen (Save on)"
29. Click "css=[role=dialog] button:text-is('Save')"
30. Audit message "reset" as "Reset dates save message"
31. Click "css=[role=dialog] button:text-is('Cancel')"
32. Wait for "css=body:not(:has([role=dialog]))"
